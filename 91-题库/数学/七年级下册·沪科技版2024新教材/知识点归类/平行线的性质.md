## 22. 

已知：如图，点\(C\)​在\(∠AOB\)​的一边\(OA\)​上，过点\(C\)​的直线\(DE//OB\)​，\(CF\)​平分\(∠ACD\)​，\(CG⊥CF\)​于\(C\)​。
​
![](../../../assets/question_resource/bfc5d06a-7449-11ed-aae6-fa20201b4500/resources/img-c46afcf3-3c3a-40a4-b1a7-f20d4c0a92f7.png)

若\(∠O=40°\)​，求\(∠ECF\)​的度数；

求证：\(CG\)​平分\(∠OCD\)​​；

当\(∠O\)​为多少度时，\(CD\)​平分\(∠OCF\)​，并说明理由。​

【答案】<div>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><latex class="math-tex">\(∵DE//OB，∴∠O=∠ACE\)</latex>​，（两直线平行，同位角相等）​</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">​<latex class="math-tex">\(∵∠O=40°，∴∠ACE=40°\)</latex>​，</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">​​​​​<latex class="math-tex">\(∵∠ACD+∠ACE=180°\)</latex>​，（平角定义）​​​​​</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">​<latex class="math-tex">\(∴∠ACD=140°\)</latex>​，</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">又<latex class="math-tex">\(∵CF\)</latex>​平分<latex class="math-tex">\(∠ACD\)</latex>​，</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">​<latex class="math-tex">\(∴∠ACF=70°\)</latex>​，（角平分线定义）​​</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;"><latex class="math-tex">\(∴∠ECF=70°+40°=110°\)</latex>​</p>
</div>
，<div>
<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;">证明：<latex class="math-tex">\(∵CG⊥CF，∴∠FCG=90°\)</latex>​，</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;">​​​<latex class="math-tex">\(∴∠DCG+∠DCF=90°\)</latex>​，</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;">又<latex class="math-tex">\(∵∠AOC=180°\)</latex>​，（平角定义）</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;">​​​<latex class="math-tex">\(∴∠GCO+∠FCA=90°\)</latex>​，</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;"><latex class="math-tex">\(∵∠ACF=∠DCF，∴∠GCO=∠GCD\)</latex>​，（等角的余角相等）</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;">即<latex class="math-tex">\(CG\)</latex>​平分<latex class="math-tex">\(∠OCD\)</latex>​。 </p>
</div>
，<div>
<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;">结论：当<latex class="math-tex">\(∠O=60°\)</latex>​时，<latex class="math-tex">\(CD\)</latex>​平分<latex class="math-tex">\(∠OCF\)</latex>​。</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;">当<latex class="math-tex">\(∠O=60°\)</latex>​时，</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;">​​​<latex class="math-tex">\(∵DE//OB\)</latex>​，</p>

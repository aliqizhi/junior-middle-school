## 10. 

已知\( \frac{p}{x-2}+\frac{q}{x-3}=\frac{x-1}{\left(x-2\right)\left(x-3\right)}\)，求常数\( p\)和\( q\)的值。

【答案】<div><p style="text-align: justify;">左式$ =\frac{p}{x-2}+\frac{q}{x-3}=\frac{p(x-3)+q(x-2)}{\left(x-2\right)\left(x-3\right)}=\frac{(p+q)x-(3p+2q)}{(x-2)(x-3)}$=右式，故$ \left\{\begin{array}{c}p+q=1\\ -(3p+2q)=-1\end{array}\right.$，解得$ \left\{\begin{array}{c}p+q=1\\ -(3p+2q)=-1\end{array}\right.$，得$ \left\{\begin{array}{c}p=-1\\ q=2\end{array}\right.$</p></div>

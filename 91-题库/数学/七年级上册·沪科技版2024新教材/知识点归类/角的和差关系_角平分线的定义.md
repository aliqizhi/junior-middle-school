## 3. （难度：适中）

如图，\(O\)为直线\(AB\)上一点，\(\angle DOE = 90^\circ \)，\(OD\)是\(\angle AOC\)的角平分线，求证：\(OE\)平分\(\angle BOC\)。

![](../../../assets/question_resource/f1952efc-81b9-11ed-aae6-fa20201b4500/resources/img-99938715-607a-4797-8da4-a3575e6c6800.png)

【答案】<div>
<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#000000">解：<span style="color:#000000">∵<span style="color:#000000">$OD$<span style="color:#000000">是<span style="color:#000000">$\angle AOC$<span style="color:#000000">的角平分线</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#000000">∴<span style="color:#000000">$\angle COD = \frac{1}{2}\angle AOC$</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#000000">∵<span style="color:#000000">$\angle AOD + \angle BOD = 180^\circ $</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#000000">$\angle BOD = 180^\circ - \angle AOD$</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#000000">∵<span style="color:#000000">$\angle DOE = 90^\circ $</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#000000">∴<span style="color:#000000">$\angle EOC = \angle EOD - \angle COD = 90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}\angle AOC$</p>

<p style="margin-left: 0.19in;text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#000000">∴<span style="color:#000000">$\angle EOC = \frac{1}{2}\angle BOC$</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="color:#000000">∴<span style="color:#000000">$OE$<span style="color:#000000">平分<span style="color:#000000">$\angle BOC$</p>

# Grammar "Gerunds" & "Gerunds as subjects"（同步练习）

> 教材：英语·八年级下册·沪教版(上海) ｜ 来源：国家中小学智慧教育平台·在线练习 ｜ 共 4 题

## 1. 单选题（难度：简单）

Our teacher said that she had a lot of difficulty _____  your handwriting.

- A. to read
- B. to reading
- C. reading
- D. read

【答案】C

【解析】固定搭配 have a lot of difficulty (in) doing sth.，意为“做某事有困难”，故选C。

## 2. 单选题（难度：简单）

My grandfather enjoys  ______ fishing when he is free.

- A. goes
- B. to go
- C. going ​​
- D. gone

【答案】C

【解析】本句考查动词enjoy后跟动名词 enjoy doing sth.，意为“享受做某事”，故选C。

## 3. 单选题（难度：简单）

Would you mind_______quiet for a moment?

- A. to keep ​​
- B. keeping
- C. keep ​
- D. keeps ​​

【答案】B

【解析】考查mind的用法mind doing sth. 意为“介意做某事”，故选B。

## 4. 单选题（难度：简单）

I’m trying_______ some skills when I have free time, for example swimming or drawing.

- A. learn
- B. to learn
- C. learning
- D. to learning ​​

【答案】C

【解析】考查动词try的两种用法，try to do something 和 try doing something. try to do something意为“尽力做某事”， try doing something意为“尝试做某事”。本句是指“当我有时间时，我正在尝试学习新技能”，故选C。

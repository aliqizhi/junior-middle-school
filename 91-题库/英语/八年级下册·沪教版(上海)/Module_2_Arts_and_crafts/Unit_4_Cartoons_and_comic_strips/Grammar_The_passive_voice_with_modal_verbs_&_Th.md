# Grammar "The passive voice with modal verbs" & "The passive voice in the present perfect tense"（同步练习）

> 教材：英语·八年级下册·沪教版(上海) ｜ 来源：国家中小学智慧教育平台·在线练习 ｜ 共 5 题

## 1. 单选题（难度：简单）

Many of Mo Yan’s works _________ into films over the past years.

- A. had been made
- B. were made
- C. would be made
- D. have been made

【答案】D

【解析】根据“over the past years”可知，本句是现在完成时；又因“works”和“make”之间存在被动关系，故此处需用现在完成时的被动语态，其结构为：have/has been done。

## 2. 单选题（难度：简单）

So far, new energy automobiles ________ by more and more people in our city.

- A. will use
- B. will be used
- C. have used
- D. have been used

【答案】D

【解析】分析句子可知，主语new energy automobiles和谓语use之间是被动关系，根据“So far”可知此处用现在完成时的被动语态have/has been done。故选D。

## 3. 单选题（难度：简单）

To make our city more beautiful, rubbish ________ into the river.

- A. needn't be thrown
- B. mustn't be thrown
- C. can't throw
- D. may not throw

【答案】B

【解析】由“To make our city more beautiful, rubbish ... into the river”语境可知，此处是指垃圾不能被扔进河里，mustn’t表示禁止，且应用被动语态。故选B。

## 4. 单选题（难度：简单）

During the Tang Dynasty, nearly everything produced in the world ________ on the streets of Chang’an.

- A. should be found
- B. could be found
- C. had been found
- D. must be found

【答案】B

【解析】根据主语“nearly everything produced in the world”与动词find之间是动宾关系，此处表示能够被找到，结合“During the Tang Dynasty”可知，用过去式could表示“能够”，此处是含情态动词的被动语态，用could be found。故选B。

## 5. 单选题（难度：简单）

—Do you think teenagers ________ to make their own decisions?

—No, I don't think so. After all, they are too young.

- A. might be encouraged
- B. might encourage
- C. should be encouraged
- D. should encourage

【答案】C

【解析】根据“teenagers”和“encourage”可知两者是被动关系，用被动语态，排除BD。might也许；should应该。根据句意可知此处是“是否应该被鼓励自己做决定”，情态动词用should。故选C。

## 1. 单选题（难度：简单）

—He wasn't careful enough_______ his work.

 —He should check it again.

- A. to do
- B. done
- C. does
- D. did

【答案】A

【解析】本题考查的是“adj.＋enough＋to do sth.”的用法，意为“足以……去做某事”,故选A。

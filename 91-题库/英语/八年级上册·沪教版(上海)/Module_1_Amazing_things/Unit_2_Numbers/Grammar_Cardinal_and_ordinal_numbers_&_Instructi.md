# Grammar"Cardinal and ordinal numbers" & "Instructions about numbers"（同步练习）

> 教材：英语·八年级上册·沪教版(上海) ｜ 来源：国家中小学智慧教育平台·在线练习 ｜ 共 3 题

## 1. 单选题（难度：简单）

My sister is _____child in our family, and she has_______sisters.

- A. the second; third
- B. the second; three
- C. two; three
- D. two; third

【答案】B

【解析】根据题意可知，”姐姐是家里第二个孩子”，故用序数词the second，第二个空表示“她有三个姐妹”，故用基数词three,选B

## 2. 单选题（难度：简单）

This is my ____________job and I've been doing it for four months.

- A. three
- B. third
- C. the three
- D. the third

【答案】B

【解析】根据句意理解，“这是我的第三个工作”，为序数词，故用third，因为前面有形容词性物主代词my, 定冠词the 省略，故选B。

## 3. 单选题（难度：简单）

— Today people use modern machines to look at the sky.

— Yes. This is very different from the situation ________years ago when people could only use their eyes.

- A. thousand
- B. thousands
- C. thousand of
- D. thousands of

【答案】D

【解析】当hundred, thousand, million这样的数词以短语的形式出现的时候，要在数词后面加“s”， thousands of 表示“数千个”，符合题意，故选D。

# Grammar "Adverbial clauses of concession with although and though"（同步练习）

> 教材：英语·九年级上册·沪教版(上海) ｜ 来源：国家中小学智慧教育平台·在线练习 ｜ 共 5 题

## 1. 单选题（难度：简单）

— How do you like your new school, Kate?

— It is very clean and beautiful ______ it is a bit small.

- A. if
- B. since
- C. unless
- D. although

【答案】D

【解析】根据语境，“它很干净漂亮，虽然很小。”，所以使用Although引导让步状语从句，故选D

## 2. 单选题（难度：简单）

_______ English has a history of thousands of years, the first English dictionary didn’t appear until the 17th century.

- A. Because
- B. Although
- C. Unless
- D. When

【答案】B

【解析】主从复合句中，使用Although引导让步状语从句，表示“虽然”，故选B。

## 3. 单选题（难度：简单）

_______ Zhong Nanshan is over 80 years old, he still works hard to help sick people.

- A. Because
- B. Though
- C. But
- D. And

【答案】B

【解析】主从复合句中，使用though引导让步状语从句，根据语境，“虽然钟南山已经八十多岁，但是他努力治病救人。”故选B。

## 4. 单选题（难度：简单）

Tom and Jerry really love each other _______ they fight a lot.

- A. if
- B. though
- C. until
- D. because

【答案】B

【解析】主从复合句中，使用though引导让步状语从句，“Tom和Jerry喜欢彼此，尽管他们经常打闹。”，故选B

## 5. 单选题（难度：简单）

— Have you heard of the news about Yuan Longping?

— What sad news! He is no longer with us, _________ he will be a role model to us all.

- A. so
- B. if
- C. but
- D. as

【答案】C

【解析】根据语境，并列复合句中使用but表示转折，故选C
